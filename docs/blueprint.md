# **App Name**: Freesia Finds POS

## Core Features:

- Dashboard Overview: Dashboard: Display sales reports with customizable date ranges and summary cards for Total Sales, Daily Sales, Stock Quantity, Total Product Cost Price, Total Profit (excluding delivery), Total Rejected Products, and Rejected Price.
- Product Listing: Product Page: List products with image, name, quantity, cost price, sales price, and options to view/edit/delete/reject items.
- Add Product: Add Product Page: Add product name, description, quantity, sales price, cost price, and product image (via image link).
- Sales Listing: Sales Page: List sales with invoice number, user name, total price, and date.
- Create Invoice: New Sales: Create invoices for customers, including customer name, phone, address, multiple product selection (with editable product names), shipping cost, and discount. Order decreases the qty; products will become unlisted when out of stock.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep violet (#4C2882) to evoke sophistication and luxury, reflecting the premium nature of Freesia Finds.
- Background color: Very light grey (#F2F0F4), creating a clean and modern canvas for the violet to stand out. 
- Accent color: Orchid pink (#D679C2) to draw attention to calls to action.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif to convey elegance. Body font: 'PT Sans', a clear sans-serif font.
- Use minimalist line icons to represent different actions and categories throughout the POS system.
- Employ a clean, grid-based layout for consistent alignment and easy navigation across all pages.
- Subtle transitions and animations when navigating between pages and when data is updated.
